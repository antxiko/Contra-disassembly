What is inside

The game

  • The stages: the nineteen stages, every map whole and a clip of each.
  • The bases: the four 3D bases: how you get around and the map of their doors.
  • The player: controls, lives, capsules, the weapon menu and every weapon firing.
  • The enemies: every enemy seen in the whole game, by type.
  • The bosses: the boss of every stage, animated.
  • The sequences: the logo, the title, the demos and the ending, animated.
  • The music: the 11 tunes, from its own sound engine.
  • The texts: every text of the cartridge, in its own lettering.

Curiosities

  • Curiosities: the stage that is skipped, the hidden weapons, the Game Master 2, what is still open.

Under the hood

  • Under the hood: rebuilding the ROM, the mapper, the interrupt, the RAM and the code.

This is documentation and preservation work: the code and artwork still belong to their authors and to Konami, and the cartridge image is not distributed.
